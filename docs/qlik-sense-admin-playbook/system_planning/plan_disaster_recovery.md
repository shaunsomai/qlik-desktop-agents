# Plan Disaster Recovery

Develop a recovery plan aligned with acceptable downtime and organizational resilience needs.

## Key points

- Agree a recovery time objective before choosing architecture.
- Consider naming, repeatable procedures, and hot, warm, or cold approaches.
- Document the plan for exercises and improvement.
